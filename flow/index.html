Team Process

Digital Asset Workflow

A structured process that ensures each team member's expertise is applied at the right stage — from concept to client delivery.

Click any step to expand it and learn more.

1
Solution Designer
Storyboard Authoring

The Solution Designer creates the storyboard, defining structure, narrative flow, and content — without adding graphics. Ideas and instructions for visuals are captured as review comments, preserving a clean foundation for the Visual Designer.

Storyboard draft No graphics included Visual notes in comments
2
Quality Control
Storyboard QC Review

Quality Control reviews the storyboard for errors, gaps, and inconsistencies before any visual work begins. Catching issues at this stage prevents rework downstream and ensures the Visual Designer has a solid, accurate brief to work from.

Content accuracy check Storyboard sign-off Feedback to team
3
Visual Designer
Visual Build

The Visual Designer brings the asset to life using the QC-approved storyboard as a blueprint. They apply their visual design expertise — layout, typography, color, and illustration — drawing on the Solution Designer's comments for guidance.

Works from approved storyboard Interprets visual comments Full design execution
4
Full Team + Client
Internal Review & Client Draft

The team conducts a final internal review pass before delivering the draft to the client. Both internal stakeholders and the client provide feedback, which is collected and consolidated for the next revision cycle.

Internal sign-off Client draft delivery Feedback consolidated
5
Solution Designer
Storyboard Updates

The Solution Designer revises the storyboard to incorporate all internal and client feedback. Updated visual instructions are added as comments where needed, giving the Visual Designer a clear, revised brief for the next build pass.

Implements all feedback Revised visual comments Updated storyboard issued
6
Visual Designer
Visual Updates

The Visual Designer applies revisions to the asset based on the updated storyboard, addressing all feedback from the internal and client review. The updated asset is then ready for any further review rounds as needed.

Revisions from updated SB Addresses all feedback Asset ready for next review
