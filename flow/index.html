Team Process

Digital Asset Workflow

A structured process that ensures each team member's expertise is applied at the right stage — from concept to client delivery.

1
Solution Designer
Storyboard Authoring

The Solution Designer creates the storyboard, defining structure, narrative flow, and content — without adding graphics. Ideas and instructions for visuals are captured as review comments, preserving a clean foundation for the Visual Designer.

Storyboard draft No graphics included Visual notes in comments
2
Visual Designer
Asset Build

The Visual Designer brings the asset to life using the approved storyboard as a blueprint. They apply their visual design expertise — layout, typography, color, and illustration — while drawing on the Solution Designer's commentary for guidance.

Works from storyboard Interprets visual comments Full design execution
3
Quality Control
QC Review

Quality Control audits the completed asset against the storyboard for errors, inconsistencies, and required corrections. Any issues are documented and communicated back to the team before the asset advances further.

Error & accuracy check Storyboard alignment Feedback to team
4
Full Team
Internal Review & Client Draft

The team conducts one final internal review pass to ensure the asset meets standards and reflects all QC feedback. Once approved internally, the draft is delivered to the client for their review.

Internal sign-off Client draft delivery Review cycle begins
