About

Art Director and Graphic Designer with 20 years of experience leading creative across elearning, product support, corporate communications, and digital campaigns. I work across illustration, animation, and design, building visual systems that are eye-catching, strategic, and thoughtful.

My background spans the life sciences industry where I developed training content, technical illustrations, product support materials, and corporate event identities. Since 2011 I've been at Performance Development Group in Malvern, PA, where I moved from Senior Graphic Designer into my current role as Art Director.

Outside work: Sports fan, gamer, beach lover, occasional aerial photographer, and sometimes disc golfer.
