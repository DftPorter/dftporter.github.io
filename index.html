01 About

Art Director and Graphic Designer with 20 years of experience leading creative across e-learning, product launches, corporate communications, and digital campaigns. I work at the intersection of illustration, animation, and design — building visual systems that are as strategic as they are considered.

My background spans the life sciences industry where I developed training content, technical illustrations, product launch materials, and corporate event identities. Since 2011 I've been at Performance Development Group in Malvern, PA, where I moved from Senior Graphic Designer into my current role as Art Director.

Outside work: dad, sports fan, gamer, occasional aerial photographer, and a firm advocate for ginger representation.
